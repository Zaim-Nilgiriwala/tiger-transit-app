<objective>
Build the glassmorphic callout bubble overlay system: tapping a bus or stop marker opens an informative glass-panel callout above the marker with live data that refreshes every poll cycle.

Purpose: CALL-01 through CALL-05 require interactive callout bubbles on bus and stop markers. These are custom overlays rendered outside MapView (not native Callout) for full control over glassmorphism, animations, and content. Only one callout can be open at a time, and it dismisses on outside tap.

Output: CalloutBubble component, bus and stop content variants, MapScreen wiring with marker onPress and dismiss behavior
</objective>
